# 06: Facilitator-Controlled Team Auto-Split & Team Challenges

**What to build:** 
Facilitators can configure target team counts and have participants automatically distributed into balanced teams. A facilitator dashboard enables manual drag-and-drop reassignment. Participants receive team badges and can participate in team-based activities where responses and earned points roll up to the team.

**Blocked by:** 01: Foundation & Ephemeral Session Joining, 05: Peer Interaction & Dedicated Peer Point Budget

**Status:** resolved

- [x] Facilitator can define team count and auto-split joined participants evenly across teams
- [x] Facilitator dashboard provides an interface to manually move participants between teams
- [x] Participants see their assigned team name and teammates in their session header
- [x] Team-scoped activities allow designated or collaborative team responses
- [x] Points awarded to individual team members or team submissions roll up to team score totals
- [x] Team creation and membership changes are recorded in the event log
