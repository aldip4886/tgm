# 09: Real-Time Badge Trigger Evaluation & Facilitator Awards

**What to build:** 
Automated badge triggers evaluate criteria (e.g. `total_points >= 100`, top contributor, first responder) immediately inside transaction hooks upon qualifying actions, triggering celebratory real-time socket animations on the participant's display. Facilitators can also manually award custom badges with personalized citations directly from the dashboard.

**Blocked by:** 08: Multi-Category Scoring Engine & Live Leaderboards

**Status:** ready-for-agent

- [ ] Qualified participants receive a badge record and real-time celebratory popup on their device
- [ ] Facilitator can view available badges and manually award a badge with a reason to any participant
- [ ] Participant profile displays all earned digital badges and achievement dates
- [ ] Badge awarding events are captured in the event log
