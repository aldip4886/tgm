# 0010: Facilitator-Controlled Team Auto-Split with Manual Rebalancing

Self-selection of teams in training environments leads to uneven cohort sizes, delayed starts, and participant friction. We decided to implement facilitator-governed team allocation where the platform automatically splits joined participants into balanced teams based on target team counts, while providing a real-time facilitator dashboard to drag-and-drop or reassign participants between teams as needed. This ensures instant setup for team challenges and whiteboards while giving facilitators authoritative cohort control.
